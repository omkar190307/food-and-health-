Understanding BMI Categories

Underweight — BMI below 18.5
May indicate nutritional deficiency or underlying health issues. Consult a healthcare provider.

Normal Weight — BMI 18.5 to 24.9
Associated with the lowest health risks. Focus on maintaining this through balanced nutrition and activity.

Overweight — BMI 25.0 to 29.9
Slightly elevated risk for metabolic conditions. Lifestyle modifications can significantly reduce risks.

Obese — BMI 30.0 and above
Increased risk for diabetes, heart disease, and joint problems. Medical guidance recommended.

⚠️ BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnostic measure. Factors like muscle mass, age, and ethnicity affect its accuracy. Always consult a healthcare professional.
